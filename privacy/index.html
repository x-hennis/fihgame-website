Privacy policy

This is a translation of the German privacy policy. If the two differ, the German version applies: https://fihgame.com/de/datenschutz/

As of: 21 November 2026

This policy applies to the website fihgame.com.

1. Who is responsible?

The controller responsible for processing personal data on this website is:

Niklas Menten Grafikdesign
Owner: Niklas Menten
Friesenstraße 30
50670 Köln (Cologne)
Germany
E-mail: info@niklasmenten.de

I have not appointed a data protection officer because I am not required to.

2. The short version

  • This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking services. It shows no advertising.
  • The only thing stored on your device is your choice in the privacy settings (section 4).
  • Fonts, pictures, videos and music are on this website itself. Something is loaded from another server only if you allow it: the trailer from YouTube (section 5).
  • There are no accounts and no contact form.

3. Hosting on GitHub Pages

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.

When you visit the website, GitHub processes the technical data needed to deliver the page and keep the service secure: your IP address, date and time, the page requested and information about your browser (server logs). I have no access to these logs and do not analyse them.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: my legitimate interest in a secure and reliably available website.

Data is transferred to the USA. According to its own information, GitHub, Inc. is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, so the transfer is based on the European Commission's adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). GitHub also uses the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. GitHub decides how long it keeps the logs. More in GitHub's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

4. Your privacy settings

On your first visit the website asks whether it may load content from YouTube. You can choose "Only necessary" or "Allow all", or decide item by item under "Settings". Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you have chosen.

We store your choice in your browser's local storage (entry "fih-consent"): whether YouTube is allowed and when you chose. The entry does not leave your device, is not a cookie and is not sent to us. It stays until you delete it or change your choice.

Storing this choice is strictly necessary so that the website can respect it (Section 25(2) no. 2 of the German TDDDG). The legal basis for the processing is Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR in conjunction with Art. 7(1) GDPR (proof of consent) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time: under "Privacy settings" at the bottom of every page. The withdrawal applies from then on; what happened before is not affected.

5. The trailer from YouTube

We show the official trailer through YouTube, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland ("Google"). We embed it in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com).

The trailer is only loaded if you allow it: in the privacy settings or with a click on "Load video" in the player. Before that, no connection to YouTube is made. Without consent you can also watch the trailer without YouTube; the website then plays its own file.

As soon as the trailer is loaded, your browser connects to Google's servers. Google then receives in particular your IP address, the address of this page, information about your browser and device and, if you play the video, how you use it. YouTube may store or read cookies and similar technologies on your device. If you are signed in to Google, Google can link the visit to your account. How Google uses this data is described in Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; for access to your device Section 25(1) TDDDG). You can withdraw it at any time in the privacy settings; the embedded player is then removed at once.

Data may be transferred to the USA, to Google LLC. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; the transfer is based on the European Commission's adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR).

6. Other videos and music

The moving start picture, the trailers "The fights" and "The eggs", the trailer without YouTube and the music are files of this website. They are not fetched from any other service. The music only plays if you switch it on.

7. Links to Steam and YouTube

The website links to Steam and to YouTube. As long as you do not follow such a link, nothing is sent to these providers. If you follow a link, the privacy policy of that provider applies there: for Steam that of Valve Corporation (https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/), for YouTube that of Google Ireland Limited (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

8. If you write to me

If you send me an e-mail, I process your message and your e-mail address in order to reply (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). I delete them when the matter is settled, unless a law obliges me to keep them longer.

9. The game FIH

FIH has not been released yet. The privacy notices for the game itself (for example about the Steam features and purchases in the game) will be published on this page and in the game at its release.

10. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access the data I process about you (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • rectification of incorrect data (Art. 16),
  • erasure (Art. 17), unless there is a duty to keep it,
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18),
  • data portability (Art. 20),
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21),
  • withdraw a consent with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)).

Write to info@niklasmenten.de for this.

You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example the authority where you live or the one responsible for me:

Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen Kavalleriestraße 2-4 40213 Düsseldorf, Germany https://www.ldi.nrw.de

11. Changes

When the website changes (for example with a new feature), I adapt this privacy policy. The current version is always on this page.
