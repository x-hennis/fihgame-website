Privacy policy

This is a translation of the German privacy policy. If the two differ, the German version applies: https://fihgame.com/de/datenschutz/

As of: 12 November 2026

This policy applies to the website fihgame.com.

1. Who is responsible?

The controller responsible for processing personal data on this website is:

Niklas Menten Grafikdesign

Friesenstraße 30
50670 Köln (Cologne)
Germany
E-mail: info@niklasmenten.de

I have not appointed a data protection officer because I am not required to.

2. In short

  • This website sets no cookies and stores nothing else on your device.
  • It uses no analytics or tracking and shows no ads.
  • It loads nothing from other servers: fonts, pictures, videos and the music are files of the website itself.
  • There is no account, no contact form and no newsletter.
  • Only the technical data without which no website can be delivered is processed (section 3).

Because the website sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device, it needs no cookie notice.

3. Hosting at GitHub Pages

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.

When you open the website, GitHub processes the technical data needed to deliver the page and to keep the service secure: your IP address, date and time, the page requested and details of your browser (server logs). I have no access to these logs and do not analyse them.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: my legitimate interest in a secure website that can be reached reliably.

In the process data is transferred to the USA. According to GitHub, GitHub, Inc. is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, so the transfer is based on the adequacy decision of the European Commission (Art. 45 GDPR). In addition GitHub uses the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. GitHub decides how long it keeps the logs. More about this in GitHub's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

4. Video and music

The moving start picture, the trailer and the music are files of this website. They are not fetched from YouTube or any other service. The music only plays when you switch it on.

5. Links to Steam

The website links to Steam. If you follow such a link, the privacy policy of Valve Corporation applies there: https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/

6. When you write to me

If you send me an e-mail, I process your message and your e-mail address in order to answer you (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). I delete them once the matter is settled, unless a law obliges me to keep them longer.

7. The game FIH

FIH has not been released yet. The privacy information for the game itself (for example on the Steam features and on purchases in the game) will be on this page and in the game when it is released.

8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access to the data I process about you (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • rectification of incorrect data (Art. 16),
  • erasure (Art. 17), as far as no duty to keep the data applies,
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18),
  • data portability (Art. 20),
  • object to processing that is based on a legitimate interest (Art. 21).

Write to info@niklasmenten.de for this.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one where you live or the one responsible for me:

Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen Kavalleriestraße 2-4 40213 Düsseldorf Germany https://www.ldi.nrw.de

If the website changes (for example through a new feature), I will update this privacy policy. The current version is always on this page.
