Dive in

Your fish do the digging

Up to six of your fish dig through the sand mounds of your reef. Under them lie coins, eggs and old finds.

Mound by mound you go deeper: through 10+ zones, from the Sunlit Shallows down to the Abyss, past mini bosses and zone bosses.

  • 400 depths
  • A boss in every zone

Every egg is a surprise

Hatch your eggs in the Hatchery and meet a new fish. Many are Common, a few are Mythic, and now and then one comes out Shiny.

The exact odds are on the screen before you hatch. And every season has its own rare seasonal fish.

  • 8 rarities
  • Odds always shown
  • A seasonal fish every season

50+ fish, each with a Shiny form

Fill your Fish-Dex, once with the normal fish and once with the Shinies.

Bond two fish of a kind to make one stronger, and put your favourites in the aquarium, where they swim through your reef.

  • 50+ species
  • 50+ Shiny forms
  • Two Dex pages to fill

Send a team out to sea

In Explore a team of three follows the treasure map through eight areas, from the Sunny Shallows to the Ancient Ocean Temple. The fish fight on their own, you pick the team.

Each area has ten stages, and the last one is a boss. Every win brings coins, and every boss opens a new area.

  • 8 areas
  • A boss in every area
  • Fights run on their own

Climb the Reef League

In the Reef League your team of three takes on other teams. Every win brings rating, and with it you climb through six leagues, from Sandy up to Leviathan.

On the way up you collect eggs, shells and coins. And with every new season the climb begins again.

  • 6 leagues
  • Rewards on the way up
  • A new climb every season

Make the reef your own

Set lighthouses, corals, old amphorae and treasure chests around your sand mounds. Many decorations bring a small bonus too.

Visit the reefs of your Steam friends and lend each other a fish.

  • Decorations with bonuses
  • Visit friends
  • Lend a fish

It goes on while you are away

Close the game and your fish keep digging for a while. When you come back, coins and eggs are waiting.

An upgrade tree with 277 nodes, daily and weekly goals and 69 achievements give you something to aim for. And with the Great Tide you start the dive again from the top, with pearls for lasting blessings.

  • 277 upgrades
  • 69 achievements
  • The Great Tide
