Reef Emperor

A giant wrasse with a proud hump on its forehead and blue green waves on its cheeks. It patrols the reef like an old king.

Did you know? Humphead wrasses grow up to two metres long and can live for thirty years.

Your fish do the digging

Up to six of your fish dig through the sand mounds of your reef. Under them lie coins, eggs and old finds.

Mound by mound you go deeper: through 10+ zones, from the Sunlit Shallows down to the Abyss, past mini bosses and zone bosses.

  • 10+ depth zones
  • 400 depths
  • A boss in every zone

Every egg is a surprise

Hatch your eggs in the Hatchery and meet a new fish. Many are Common, a few are Mythic, and now and then one comes out Shiny.

The exact odds are on the screen before you hatch. And every season has its own rare seasonal fish.

  • 8 rarities
  • Odds always shown
  • A seasonal fish every season

A fish for every season

Four rare fish come only in their own season. Every egg has a small chance for the fish of the season, and it is always a little stronger than your best other fish.

Psst! These four only come in their own season.

Winter

Wintervail

A dragon koi of the winter sea with a crown of red coral and veils for fins. It only comes in the cold season, and only to the lucky.

Spring

Blossomwake

A gentle whale shark of the spring sea, its spots grown over with moss and cherry blossoms. It drifts in only while the blossoms fall.

Summer

Sunwing

A manta of the summer sea, its wings glowing gold and orange like the evening sun on the water. It only glides in while the days are long.

Autumn

Emberleaf

A leafy seadragon of the autumn sea, hung with leaves of red, orange and gold. It only drifts in while the leaves fall.

50+ fish, each with a Shiny form

Fill your Fish-Dex, once with the normal fish and once with the Shinies. Every fish is its own detailed 3D model and swims in its own way.

Bond two fish of a kind to make one stronger, and put your favourites in the aquarium, where they swim through your reef.

  • 50+ species
  • 50+ Shiny forms
  • Two Dex pages to fill

Send your team on an expedition

In Explore a team of up to five fish follows the treasure map through eight areas, from the Sunny Shallows to the Ancient Ocean Temple. Every fish fights in its own way: sharks bite, rays sweep in, crabs pinch.

Before every fight you pick a tactic, and every fish has an element: Ember melts Frost, Frost wilts Bloom, Bloom smothers Ember. Each area has ten stages, and the last one is a boss.

  • 8 areas
  • Up to 5 fish in a team
  • Elements and tactics

Climb the Reef League

In the Reef League your team takes on other teams. Every win brings rating, and with it you climb through six leagues, from Sandy up to Leviathan.

On the way up you collect eggs, shells and coins. And with every new season the climb begins again.

  • 6 leagues
  • Rewards on the way up
  • A new climb every season
  1. Sandy
  2. Shell
  3. Coral
  4. Pearl
  5. Abyssal
  6. Leviathan

Make the reef your own

Set lighthouses, corals, old amphorae and treasure chests around your sand mounds. Many decorations bring a small bonus too.

Visit the reefs of your Steam friends and lend each other a fish.

Your reef never sleeps

An upgrade tree with 277 nodes, daily and weekly goals and 68 achievements give you something to aim for. And with the Great Tide you start the dive again from the top, with pearls for lasting blessings.

  • 277 upgrades

Questions and answers

What is FIH?

FIH (A Little Ocean of Friends) is a cozy idle game about fish for PC. Your fish dig through the sand of your reef on their own, you hatch eggs, collect 50+ kinds of fish and decorate your reef. The reef keeps growing while you are away.

What kind of game is FIH?

It is a relaxed idle and collecting game, a cozy game. There is no time pressure and nothing to lose. You can play for a few minutes or leave it open beside whatever else you do.

Is FIH free?

Yes. FIH is free to play and has no ads. Every fish and every Shiny can be had by playing. There is a shop for players who want to save time or support the game, and nothing in it is needed.

Where and when can I play FIH?

FIH comes to Steam for Windows PCs. There is no release date yet, and the Steam page is on its way.

How many fish are there in FIH?

More than 50 kinds in 8 rarities, from Common to Ancient, and every season brings a seasonal fish of its own. Every fish comes out of an egg, and you see the exact chances before you hatch one.

What is a Shiny?

A Shiny is a rare form of a fish with its own colours. About 1 in 512 eggs hatches one. Shinies are for collecting: they are no stronger than normal fish, and they have their own page in the Fish-Dex.

What do you do in FIH?

Your fish dig down through 10+ depth zones, past bosses. You hatch eggs, collect your fish and make them stronger, send a team of up to five through the areas of Explore, climb the Reef League and decorate your reef.

Can I play FIH with a controller?

Yes. FIH plays with mouse and keyboard or with an Xbox or PlayStation controller. A small bar on the screen always shows what each button does.

Which languages does FIH have?

English, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Japanese, Korean and Chinese (Simplified).

Who makes FIH?

FIH is made by an independent developer from Cologne in Germany.
